## 2. The instrument — the dominant finding

The ADS closed at a **+41.1% premium to its own underlying** (2026-09-11). The Luxembourg GDS, same issuer, same depositary (Citibank N.A.), same underlying share, closed at **+0.78%** the same day. Across 50 sessions Luxembourg averaged +1.29%; the two premium distributions **do not overlap**. The GDS has traded at parity for two decades — T1 confirmation at −0.24% on 2006-06-23 from the issuer's own offering circular.

**Mechanism, at T1.** Both facilities sit under the same Korean consent regime but are keyed to different benchmarks. ADS: consent needed if a deposit exceeds "the number of common shares **initially deposited by us**" — created July 2026, nothing withdrawn, headroom ≈ **zero**. GDS: consent needed only if delivered GDSs exceed "the shares **already withdrawn from the GDS facility**" — a revolving benchmark, and the facility has bled from ~93.1m issued to 13,729,041 listed, leaving ~**79.4m shares** of consent-free headroom (well-supported inference, not a disclosed number).

So **creating ADSs — the leg that closes a premium — is unavailable, not slow or expensive.** Fees are 0.037% each way; the premium is ~555x frictional cost. Withdrawal is uncapped but realises a 29.1% loss, so no holder does it and headroom never appears.

**Not a Korean-regime feature:** KB −0.34%, LPL −0.42%, KEP −0.93%, WF +0.36%, PKX +1.07%. TSM at +13.60% is the partially-constrained middle case. SKHY at 41.1% is ~40x the Korean comparables.

**Direction is now settled:** US implies $1,904/share, Luxembourg $1,360. **SKHY is ~40% rich; Seoul is not cheap.** The sell-side gap of +29.9% on the ADS against +76.5% on KRX is one view plus a 41-point measurement error.

⚠ **Dated risk:** the company can raise the quota unilaterally, but is contractually barred from even *announcing* an intention until **2026-10-08** — three weeks before the estimated Q3 call, where it has already promised further capital-return detail. The clean negative on expansion (no F-3/F-1/F-6 amendment/shelf across 33 EDGAR filings; no 증권신고서 or DR resolution on DART) is therefore **expected, not informative**.

**Information edge:** the F-6 registered 1,780,000,000 ADSs, 9.99% utilised. A Shinhan analyst reasoned from that to "~22.5% additional issuance capacity" and concluded the premium must collapse (T4). **The SEC registration authorises the depositary to issue; Korean law governs whether shares may be deposited.** Published sell-side is wrong about this instrument's single most important feature.

## 5. Business — the LTA question, resolved

**The LTAs are neither price-committed nor volume-committed.** T1 on converging evidence.

Confirmed verbatim: "Concluded around 10 LTA negotiations including key customers" and "Financial mechanisms(e.g. deposits) incorporated to support contract fulfillment" (2Q26 presentation p.10, both languages).

🛑 **"Typically five-year terms" is unsupported** — absent from the slide, both languages of the press release, and the Semi-Annual Report. Korean says only 다년 (multi-year). **Unverifiable rather than disproven**: no transcript is published (script PDF 404s, link hidden with `display:none`; audio only). The ledger's P-002 detail carried this as fact; a correction is appended there.

**The decisive T1 fact:** the report filed **2026-08-14**, sixteen days *after* the announcement, **newly appended** to its Order Status schedule: 장기공급계약에 따른 수주 현황은 없습니다 — "there is no order status arising from long-term supply contracts." Absent from both the FY2025 (2026-03-17) and Q1 (2026-05-15) filings. FSS Art. 4-2-7 expressly brings 장기매출계약 **within scope** and demands order total and backlog in quantity and value, so no backlog means no determinable committed quantity or price. The company also did not invoke the trade-secret carve-out, which the same rule forecloses once content is public — and it was, on 2026-07-29.

**Deposits are invisible at scale.** Contract liabilities ₩523,532M + advance receipts ₩49,582M = ₩573,114M at 2026-06-30 = **0.72% of one quarter's revenue**, about **0.66 days**. *Fell* 5.3% sequentially while revenue rose 51%, and was larger at 2024-12-31 (₩543,477M) — not an LTA line. No non-current contract liabilities, no refund liability. Point-in-time revenue 99.955%.

⚠ Caveat: all pre-date 2026-07-29. **LTA deposits would first appear in the Q3 report (~2026-11-16 `(est.)`)** — the first Order Status schedule dated after signing, and the print that closes this either way.

Management has never used the word **binding**. Pricing described only as "designed to address price volatility" and "varying by customer" — no index, floor, collar or formula. English/Korean divergence runs one way: English "has finalized Long-Term Agreements" against Korean 협상을 마무리했으며, finalized the *negotiations*.

## 6. Capital allocation — coherent, and the reason is statutory

The raise-then-buyback looks incoherent until you see the constraint. The 424B4 states the offering was **size-capped by law** to keep SK Square above the holding-company 20% floor. SK Square holds 146,100,000 shares; post-listing issued shares are 730,492,365, and 20% of that is 146,098,473. **Margin: 1,527 shares. Stake: 20.0002%.** The IPO consumed the entire compliance buffer to four decimals. Cancelling 24,070,000 shares restores ~4.8m shares of issuance capacity — and the company did exactly this in February, disposing 15,300,000 treasury shares (footnote explains the 20.07% → 20.50% move).

🛑 **But the 24,070,000 share count is a placeholder, not a commitment — corrected 2026-09-11.** The cancellation 6-K discloses its own derivation: the count *"was calculated by dividing the 'Estimated Aggregate Cancellation Value (Won)' … by the closing price of the Company's common shares on the day prior to the date of the resolution by the Board of Directors (August 18, 2026: Won 1,662,000). **However, the actual number of shares cancelled and the cancellation amount may change depending on movements in the Company's share price during the treasury share acquisition process.**"* Check it: ₩1,662,000 × 24,070,000 = ₩40,004,340,000,000 exactly.

**So the won amount is the commitment and the share count floats inversely with the price.** At the 2026-09-11 close of ₩1,812,000 the same ₩40,004,340,000,000 retires **22,077,450 shares — 1,992,550 fewer, or 8.3% less shrinkage than the headline**, and the error grows as the stock rises. **Anyone modelling a fixed 24.07M reduction is over-crediting the programme.** The 6-K also says the company *"plans to cancel all shares acquired in a single batch after the completion of the acquisition,"* so nothing lands before 2026-11-19.

Sizing context, all T1 from the same filings: the legal ceiling on treasury purchases is **₩89,448,011,686,863**, so ₩40.0tn uses **44.7% of capacity**; the maximum daily purchase order is 2,407,000 shares; **15,300,000 shares were already cancelled earlier in 2026**, leaving just 1,625,769 treasury shares at 2026-08-19. The shareholder-return 6-K commits to returning *"over 50% of cumulative Free Cash Flow"* across 2025–2027, with further detail *"announced at the time of the third quarter earnings announcement"* — which is why the late-October print matters.

Funding is not a constraint: the buyback is **60.9%** of a *single quarter's* operating cash flow (₩40,004bn ÷ ₩65,710bn), against ₩69.37tn net cash. New fab approvals (M17 ₩19.1tn, Yongin Fab 2 ₩35.2tn, P&T7 ₩7.1tn) run to 2031-32, ~₩11tn/yr against ₩65.7tn quarterly OCF.

⚠ Two reservations: the buyback is **100.29%** of cash actually raised, uncomfortably tight to be coincidence; and **no filing states the threshold motive** — the stated rationale is undervaluation. Well-supported inference, not disclosed.

**Execution is unanswerable, not negative.** No progress filing exists and none is required until after the acquisition period closes **2026-11-19**. A T4 claim of ~29% complete is unsupported. The only treasury filing in the window disposed **82 shares** to independent directors — a governance formality.
